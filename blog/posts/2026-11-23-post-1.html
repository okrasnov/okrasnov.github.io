Radar Publications Weekly Briefing (17–23 November 2026)

Polarimetry

  1. A version of the Cloude/van Zyl decomposition for full/quad pol synthetic aperture radar data
    Presents a full/quad-polarisation version of the Cloude/van Zyl decomposition that yields lower entropy and clearer dominant scattering mechanisms for bright man-made targets, demonstrated on X-band F-SAR data.
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26195 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.26195

  2. Generation of Sample Complex Wishart Distributed Matrices and Change Detection in Polarimetric SAR Data
    Provides a test statistic and associated probability measure for detecting differences between complex Wishart-distributed polarimetric SAR covariance matrices, validated on generated samples.
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22943 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.22943

FMCW Radars

  1. Radar-Native Control Barrier Functions: Doppler Range Rate as a Measured Barrier Derivative
    Uses direct Doppler range-rate measurements from FMCW radar as the derivative of an ego-centered range control barrier function to enable safer closed-form robot velocity filtering with reduced delay.
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23654 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.23654

Target Detection

  1. Target Detection with Tightly-coupled Wideband Antenna Arrays
    Analyzes target detection performance of tightly-coupled wideband antenna arrays under mutual coupling and derives novel detectors based on local averaging for slowly varying signals.
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20487 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.20487

  1. Early UAV Detection Using 5G NR LEO Downlinks for Passive ISAC
    Demonstrates passive integrated sensing and communication using 5G NR LEO downlinks for earlier UAV detection while matching exhaustive-search final accuracy.
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20859 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.20859

  2. QPAFusion: Query-Guided Radar Refinement and Prior-Guided Adaptive Fusion for Marine Object Detection
    Introduces query-guided local radar sampling and adaptive feature aggregation that improves marine object detection accuracy on the WaterScenes dataset, especially under adverse lighting and weather.
    Link: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/18/22/3965 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/rs18223965

  3. Hermitian Positive Definite Neural Networks for Matrix-CFAR Geometric Signal Detection in Nonhomogeneous Clutter
    Integrates HPD neural networks into the matrix-CFAR framework to learn geometry-preserving covariance estimates, reducing computational cost while maintaining detection performance in nonhomogeneous clutter.
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19820 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.19820

Other Topics

  1. Radar Waveform Design via Convex Semidefinite Relaxation
    Formulates radar waveform design for a prescribed ambiguity-function magnitude as a convex semidefinite program in a lifted space, guaranteeing global optimality up to trivial ambiguities.
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20179 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.20179

  2. DOA Estimation in Antenna Arrays: Super-Resolution Methods in a Common Space
    Unifies classical super-resolution DOA methods (Capon, MUSIC, ESPRIT and others) within a single linear-prediction and entropy-based framework applicable to Gaussian and non-Gaussian signals.
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24198 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.24198

  3. Parameter-Efficient Adaptation of Self-supervised Vision Backbones Survives Distribution Shift in Radar Micro-Doppler HAR
    Shows that parameter-efficient fine-tuning of self-supervised vision backbones enables robust micro-Doppler human activity recognition under cross-age, cross-orientation and cross-dataset shifts.
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25874 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.25874

SAR Papers (placed last)

  1. From very fine Vertical Resolution to Range Super-Resolution on BIOMASS P-BAND SAR Data
    Achieves more than twelvefold slant-range resolution improvement on BIOMASS P-band SAR tomography data over arid regions by exploiting InSAR wavenumber shift and residual topography correction.
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21358 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.21358

  2. SAR-TopoGuard: Training-Free, Speckle-Aware Topological Verification for Structure-Preserving SAR-to-Optical Translation
    Adds a zero-parameter inference-time layer that ranks generator candidates by gradient-magnitude correlation and SAR-guided frequency gating to reduce hallucinations in SAR-to-optical translation.
    Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19316 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.19316

Additional relevant works appearing in the same period include RadarML (multi-channel over-the-air radar waveform dataset), Radar Panoptic Transformer for automotive radar scene understanding, and various micro-Doppler and waveform-optimization studies.
